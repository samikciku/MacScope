# Code of Conduct

MacScope contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free.

Be respectful, discuss technical decisions in good faith, give actionable feedback, and avoid personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
